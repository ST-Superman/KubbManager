## About Page

### Our Mission

Kubb Manager was created by passionate Kubb players who recognized the need for a comprehensive training companion. Our mission is to help players of all skill levels improve their game through technology, data, and structured practice.

### The Story Behind Kubb Manager

Kubb, the ancient Viking game, has been played for over 1,000 years. Yet, until now, there was no comprehensive way to track progress, analyze performance, or structure training sessions. Kubb Manager changes that.

Our team of developers and Kubb enthusiasts spent years perfecting the app, ensuring it captures the essence of the game while providing modern training tools. From beginners learning their first throws to competitive players preparing for tournaments, Kubb Manager serves the entire community.

### Why We Built This

**The Problem**: Traditional Kubb training lacked structure, progress tracking, and detailed analytics. Players had no way to measure improvement or identify areas for development.

**The Solution**: Kubb Manager provides comprehensive training tools, detailed analytics, and multiple training modes to help players reach their full potential.

**The Impact**: Players worldwide are now improving their skills faster, tracking their progress more effectively, and enjoying the game more than ever before.
