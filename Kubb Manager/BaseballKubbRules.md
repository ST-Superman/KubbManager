# Baseball Kubb Rules

## Game Concept and Credit

**Baseball Kubb** was created by **Andy Terveer** who developed the concept and rules for this innovative variation of traditional Kubb. This game combines elements of baseball with the throwing skills required in Kubb, creating an engaging format that is a lot of fun to play!

**Learn more from the creator:** [Andy Terveer's Baseball Kubb Playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLeqH7gfCa66ptEKuilFX_wlmpeFYbcvJO)

---

## Equipment Setup

### Required Equipment
- **10 Kubbs total**: 5 for each team's baseline
- **1 King Kubb**: Placed at the centerline
- **6 Batons**: Per half-inning (unlimited in 9th inning)
- **Field Kubbs**: Variable number (calculated from previous half-inning performance)

### Pitch Layout
- Standard Kubb pitch dimensions
- Away team baseline (throws first)
- Home team baseline
- King placed at centerline
- 5 Baseline kubbs set along each baseline

---

## Game Structure

### Basic Format
- **9 Innings** total
- Each inning has **2 half-innings**:
  - **Top half**: Away team throws
  - **Bottom half**: Home team throws
- Teams alternate throwing each half-inning

### Team Designation
- **Away Team**: Always throws first (top of each inning)
- **Home Team**: Always throws second (bottom of each inning)
- A traditional king toss is used to determine Home and Away teams
- Home team has advantage of potential walk-off win

---

## Scoring System

### Runs (Primary Scoring)
- **1 Run** = 1 baseline kubb knocked down
- Runs are scored immediately when baseline kubbs are hit
- Each team tracks their total runs throughout the game

### Kings (Tiebreaker Scoring)
- **1 King Point** = 1 king kubb knocked down
- King hits are worth **1 run + 1 king point**
- Kings are used as tiebreakers if teams are tied after 9 innings
- King can only be hit after ALL other kubbs are cleared

---

## Throwing Rules

### Half-Inning Structure
- Each half-inning consists of **6 batons** (innings 1-8)
- **9th inning**: Unlimited batons until 3 misses
- Half-inning ends when:
  - 6 batons are thrown (innings 1-8), OR
  - 3 misses are recorded (any inning)

### Hit Priority System
1. **Field Kubbs First**: Must clear all field kubbs before hitting baseline kubbs
2. **Baseline Kubbs Second**: Can only hit baseline kubbs when no field kubbs remain
3. **King Last**: Can only hit king when ALL other kubbs are cleared

### Valid Throws
- **Underarm throws only**
- **Vertical rotation required**
- **No sideways or horizontal tosses**
- **No "helicopter" spins**

---

## Field Kubb System

### Field Kubb Calculation
Field kubbs for the next half-inning are calculated based on the previous half-inning's performance:

**If King was hit in previous half:**
- Field kubbs = Only kubbs hit AFTER the king reset

**If no King was hit:**
- Field kubbs = Field kubbs cleared + baseline kubbs hit

### Field Kubb Placement
- Field kubbs are placed in the field area
- They must be cleared before baseline kubbs can be hit
- Uncleared field kubbs are added to the throwing team's baseline at half-inning end

---

## Game Flow

### Starting a Game
1. Away team throws first (top of 1st inning)
2. No field kubbs at start of game
3. Each team starts with 5 baseline kubbs

### Half-Inning Process
1. **Throw Phase**: Team throws up to 6 batons (or until 3 misses in 9th)
2. **Hit Recording**: Track field kubbs hit, baseline kubbs hit, and king hits
3. **Score Update**: Runs and kings are added to team totals
4. **Field Kubb Calculation**: Determine field kubbs for next half-inning
5. **Half-Inning End**: Move to next half-inning

### Inning Transitions
- After bottom half: Move to top of next inning
- Field kubbs carry over to next half-inning
- Field kubbs are inkast by the "at-bat" team and are stood up by the defending team
- If an inkast kubb cannot be stood up in bounds then it becomes a penalty kubb (traditional kubb rule)

---

## Special Rules

### 9th Inning Rules
- **Unlimited batons** until 3 misses
- **Walk-off win**: Home team wins immediately if they take the lead in bottom 9th
- **No extra innings**: Game ends after 9 innings unless tied

### King Hit Effects
- **Immediate reset**: All kubbs return to baselines (5 on each side and the king kubb is stood back up)
- **Score tracking**: Only kubbs hit AFTER king reset count toward the next half inning's field kubbs

### Miss Consequences
- **3 strikes rule**: Half-inning ends after 3 misses
- **Uncleared field kubbs**: Added to throwing team's baseline 
**There is no advantage line in baseball kubb** - If a team fails to knock down all field kubbs on their turn, those kubbs are then added to the baseline that team is attacking.  The defending team does not need to inkast those kubbs.
- **No penalty runs**: Misses don't affect opponent's score

---

## Winning Conditions

### Regular Win
- Team with most runs after 9 innings wins
- If tied in runs, team with most kings wins
- If still tied, game continues (extra innings)

### Walk-off Win
- Home team wins immediately if leading after bottom 9th
- Can occur mid-inning if home team takes lead

### Tie Game
- If tied after 9 innings, continue playing.  Three strike rule applies in extra innings
- First team to lead after a complete inning wins

## Training Benefits

### Skill Development
- **Accuracy improvement**: Consistent throwing at different targets
- **Strategic thinking**: Planning throws based on game situation
- **Pressure handling**: Performing under game-like conditions
- **Distance control**: Varying throw power for different targets

### Game Preparation
- **Real game simulation**: Practice with game-like pressure
- **Score management**: Learn to play with different leads/deficits
- **Endurance training**: Multiple innings build throwing stamina
- **Mental toughness**: Handling misses and maintaining focus
