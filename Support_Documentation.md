# Kubb Manager Support Documentation

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### General Questions

**Q: What is Kubb Manager?**
A: Kubb Manager is a comprehensive iOS training companion for Kubb players. It helps you track your progress, improve your accuracy, and master different training modes including 8-Meter Training, Inkast & Blast, and Baseball Kubb.

**Q: What devices are supported?**
A: Kubb Manager requires iOS 18.5 or later and is optimized for iPhone and iPad. It supports all current iPhone models and iPad models.

**Q: Is the app free?**
A: Kubb Manager is free to download with optional in-app purchases for premium features.

**Q: Do I need an internet connection to use the app?**
A: No, Kubb Manager works offline. Your data is stored locally and syncs to iCloud when you have an internet connection.

### Training Features

**Q: What training modes are available?**
A: Kubb Manager offers three main training modes:
- **8-Meter Training**: Practice precision throwing with customizable targets
- **Inkast & Blast Training**: Master advanced throwing techniques
- **Baseball Kubb**: Experience the innovative Baseball Kubb variation

**Q: How do I start a training session?**
A: Open the app, go to the Training tab, select your desired training mode, and tap "Start New Session." Set your daily target and begin practicing.

**Q: Can I track my progress over time?**
A: Yes, Kubb Manager provides comprehensive progress tracking including accuracy trends, session history, and performance analytics.

**Q: What statistics are tracked?**
A: The app tracks accuracy, total kubbs hit, batons thrown, training streaks, handicap calculations, and detailed performance metrics.

### Data and Synchronization

**Q: How does data synchronization work?**
A: Kubb Manager uses Apple's CloudKit to automatically sync your data across all your iOS devices. Your data is stored securely in your iCloud account.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes, your data is encrypted in transit and at rest using Apple's CloudKit infrastructure. We never share your personal information with third parties.

**Q: Can I export my data?**
A: Yes, you can export your training data in JSON or CSV format through the app settings.

**Q: What happens if I delete the app?**
A: Your data will remain in your iCloud account. If you reinstall the app and sign in with the same Apple ID, your data will be restored.

### Baseball Kubb

**Q: What is Baseball Kubb?**
A: Baseball Kubb is an innovative variation of traditional Kubb created by Andy Terveer. It features 9-inning gameplay with strategic scoring and advanced throwing scenarios.

**Q: How is Baseball Kubb different from traditional Kubb?**
A: Baseball Kubb uses a 9-inning format with runs and kings for scoring, strategic field kubb management, and unlimited batons in the 9th inning.

**Q: Where can I learn more about Baseball Kubb?**
A: You can learn more from the creator's YouTube playlist: [Andy Terveer's Baseball Kubb Playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLeqH7gfCa66ptEKuilFX_wlmpeFYbcvJO)

### Technical Issues

**Q: The app is crashing. What should I do?**
A: Try restarting the app and your device. If the problem persists, check for app updates in the App Store. Contact support if the issue continues.

**Q: My data isn't syncing. How can I fix this?**
A: Ensure you're signed into iCloud and have an internet connection. Check your iCloud storage space. Try signing out and back into iCloud.

**Q: The app is running slowly. What can I do?**
A: Close other apps to free up memory, restart your device, and ensure you have the latest version of the app installed.

**Q: I'm having trouble with notifications.**
A: Check your device's notification settings for Kubb Manager. Ensure the app has permission to send notifications.

### Account and Settings

**Q: How do I change my training preferences?**
A: Go to the Options menu in the app to adjust your training settings, reminders, and preferences.

**Q: Can I set training reminders?**
A: Yes, you can enable training reminders and set your preferred frequency and time in the app settings.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: You can delete your data through the app settings. This will remove your data from both the app and iCloud.

### Troubleshooting

**Q: The app won't open.**
A: Try force-closing the app and reopening it. If that doesn't work, restart your device. Check if you have enough storage space.

**Q: My statistics seem incorrect.**
A: Check if you have the latest app version. Try refreshing the statistics by pulling down on the statistics screen.

**Q: I can't find my training sessions.**
A: Check the History tab to view all your training sessions. Use the filter options to find specific sessions.

**Q: The app is using too much battery.**
A: Close the app when not in use, reduce background app refresh, and ensure you have the latest app version.

### Privacy and Security

**Q: What data does the app collect?**
A: The app collects your training data, performance statistics, and usage information. All data is stored securely using Apple's CloudKit.

**Q: Is my data shared with third parties?**
A: No, we never share your personal information or training data with third parties. Your data remains under your control.

**Q: Can I control what data is collected?**
A: Yes, you can control data collection through the app settings and your device's privacy settings.

### Updates and Features

**Q: How often is the app updated?**
A: We regularly update the app with new features, improvements, and bug fixes. Updates are available through the App Store.

**Q: What new features are coming?**
A: We're working on additional training modes, enhanced analytics, and community features. Follow our updates for the latest information.

**Q: How can I suggest new features?**
A: Contact us through the app's support section or email us directly with your suggestions.
