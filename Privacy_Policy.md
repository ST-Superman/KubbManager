## Data Storage and Synchronization

### CloudKit Integration
Kubb Manager uses Apple's CloudKit service to synchronize your data across your iOS devices. This means:
- Your training data is stored securely in your personal iCloud account
- Data syncs automatically between your devices
- You can access your data from any device signed into your Apple ID
- Data is encrypted in transit and at rest

### Local Storage
The App also stores data locally on your device using Core Data:
- Data is stored securely on your device
- App functions offline without internet connection
- Data syncs to CloudKit when connection is available

### Data Retention
- Your training data is retained as long as you use the App
- You can delete your data at any time through the App settings
- Data may be retained for a reasonable period after account deletion for legal and operational purposes
